## IT 520: Foundations of IT

- Source lineage: `it520-syllabus-brief#sections.course`
- Citations: `syllabus_offering`

### Fall 2026 · 4 credits · Computer Science

Required for the Information Technology B.S. and taken in the second-year fall. Also available as an IT cognate course.

- **What**: Learn to look at a running workload and say where its time and money go.
- **How**: Defend one change with a measurement, from the machine map down to cost and reliability.

## Learning outcomes 1-3 of 8.

- Source lineage: `it520-syllabus-brief#sections.outcomes`
- Citations: `syllabus_offering`

### Course learning outcomes

| Outcome |
| --- |
| Locate a workload on the machine map, placing its steps across program, runtime, operating system, CPU and GPU, memory, storage, and network, and name the layer a stated symptom must live in. |
| Measure a bottleneck and classify it as compute-, memory-, or I/O-bound, citing the measurement as evidence and re-measuring after one change. |
| Predict and diagnose encoded state, naming the encoding rule that explains a wrap, signedness, rounding, text-encoding, byte-order, or format defect. |

## Learning outcomes 4-6 of 8.

- Source lineage: `it520-syllabus-brief#sections.outcomes_2`
- Citations: `syllabus_offering`

### Course learning outcomes

| Outcome |
| --- |
| Use discrete structures as systems machinery, stating the proposition or invariant that must hold, defining the governing set and mapping, and producing an actual count with units. |
| Predict data-movement cost across the memory hierarchy and defend the prediction with locality, cache behavior, address translation, storage tier, or serialization cost. |
| Choose and defend a CPU parallel execution strategy, predicting the Amdahl-bounded ceiling and naming the shared-state hazard and its remedy. |

## Learning outcomes 7-8 of 8.

- Source lineage: `it520-syllabus-brief#sections.outcomes_3`
- Citations: `syllabus_offering`

### Course learning outcomes

| Outcome |
| --- |
| Operate a workload under failure, isolation, and cost, specifying retry, timeout, and idempotency with the invariant that makes the retry safe. |
| Diagnose a production workload and defend one measured change, carrying a machine map, bottleneck evidence, a parallelism judgment, before-and-after measurement, and one cost, reliability, or security trade-off. |
