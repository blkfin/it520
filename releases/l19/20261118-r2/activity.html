5 · Check together and explain (16–20 minutes)

Compare your list with another group. If your totals differ, find a specific missing or repeated committee. Record one check you made.

In your own words: why does counting committees give fewer results than counting ordered picks? Use one committee from your list as evidence.

Finished early? Each committee of three leaves two candidates out. List the pairs left out and explain how they check your committee list.
