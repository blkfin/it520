The whole chain: decimal → binary → normalized 1.xxx × 2e → sign | exponent | mantissa → 32 bits

Sign (bit 31): 0 positive, 1 negative. Exponent (bits 30–23): stored as e + 127 (the bias). Mantissa (bits 22–0): the bits after the leading 1; the 1 is hidden, not stored.

Decode: value = (−1)sign × 1.mantissa × 2(stored exponent − 127)
